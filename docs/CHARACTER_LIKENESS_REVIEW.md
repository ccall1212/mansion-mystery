animation sets. They permit review of faces, clothing, proportions and duplicates
before individual game assets are generated. Do not feed a review board directly
into gameplay. Existing runtime assets remain in place during this review.

those roles after user review; do not silently finalize a larger cast or count one
person twice because they occur in multiple photographs. Original four pose-test
